Scope can creep because requirements can change a lot.
Planning and estimating long term can be harder because priorities shift.

Runs Scrum events and keeps the team following the process.

Sprint Planning
Points to discuss
What work will be done this sprint and what the sprint goal is.
How the team will approach the work and what tasks are needed.
Daily Scrum
Points to discuss
Progress toward the sprint goal and what is planned next.
Any blockers that could stop progress.
Sprint Review
Points to discuss
Demo what was completed and what was not completed.
Get stakeholder feedback and update the backlog priorities.


Epic
A large body of work that groups multiple stories. It is usually too big for one sprint.
Story
A user focused feature request. It delivers user value and can be completed in a short time.
Bug
A defect where something is broken or incorrect. It needs a fix.
Task
A piece of work that may not be user facing, like setup, refactoring, or a small technical job.
